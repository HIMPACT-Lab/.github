HIMPACT Lab is part of the School of Civil and Environmental Engineering (SCENE) at IIT Mandi, Himachal Pradesh. We study how a changing climate affects water resources in the Himalaya. We combine hydrological and atmospheric models, remote sensing and deep learning to build early-warning and decision-support tools for mountain communities.

**Lead:** [Dr. Vivek Gupta](https://scholar.google.co.in/citations?user=bMLz1QgAAAAJ&hl=en), Assistant Professor, Water Resources Engineering, IIT Mandi

### What we work on
- **Climate change impact assessment:** CMIP6 projections of precipitation and temperature extremes
- **Droughts & climate extremes:** drought propagation, ENSO teleconnections, compound extremes
- **Early warning & climate information:** forecasts turned into timely, usable guidance
- **Hydrological modelling:** SWAT and data-driven models for floods and droughts in mountain basins
- **Coupled atmosphere–hydrology:** tracing extreme rainfall through to runoff and flooding
- **AI for water:** deep learning for peak-flow estimation, time-series forecasting and remote sensing
